About Us

Leading the Way in International Education

Founded in 2016, 21Knowledge brings over 20 years of expertise in education and Erasmus+ innovation. Our founder coordinated an Erasmus+ KA1 project that was recognized as an Inspirational Project in 2016. With the motto “Learning for the 21st Century,” we continue to bridge tradition with forward-thinking approaches to empower teachers across Europe.

🌍

Mission

To empower educators and learners across Europe by providing high-quality, expert-led Erasmus+ training experiences that foster innovation, collaboration, and lifelong learning.

👁️

Vision

To become a leading force in international education, connecting a global community of learners and educators through transformative, future-ready programs that shape the 21st-century classroom.

💡

Our Core Values

  • Quality Education – We deliver impactful, research-based training led by experienced professionals.
  • Innovation – We embrace new ideas, technologies, and methodologies to enhance learning.
  • Inclusivity – We create accessible and welcoming learning environments for all.
  • Recognition – We provide accredited certifications that are valued across Europe and beyond.
